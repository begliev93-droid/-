RIGHT HALF: close-up of the young Kazakh hero from the reference (round face, big round white eyes with black pupils, rosy cheeks, tall white felt kalpak hat with black brim, red chapan with gold trim and gold belt). BACKGROUND BEHIND HIM: a wide golden steppe at sunset, orange-to-gold sky. On the horizon, a blizzard, and in it EIGHT ghostly grey translucent silhouettes of grown men (brothers) in fur hats, walking away into the snow, half-faded.

High contrast, the hero pops from the background, soft rim light on the hero, slight vignette. NO text, NO letters, NO logos, NO watermark.
